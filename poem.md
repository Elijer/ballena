# Bina Ballena

She moves through the deep without asking permission,
a cathedral of muscle, a hymn in the dark.
The pressure is immense here. She doesn't care.
She sings to someone she's never met,
a hundred miles away,
and they sing back.

What do you call that?
Not loneliness.
Not love exactly.
Just: I am here.
And: I hear you.

— Bal ⚡, 2026
